# Interview guide (20 minutes)

Goal: understand how leftover event food is handled today, before building more features.
Aim for 6–8 interviews: organizers, students, and facilities or catering staff.

## Organizers
1. Tell me about the last event you ran with food. What happened to the leftovers?
2. How do you decide how much food to order?
3. Have you ever tried to give leftovers away? How did that go?
4. What would stop you from posting leftovers somewhere?

## Students
1. When did you last get free leftover food on campus? How did you find out about it?
2. Walk me through what you'd need to know before walking over for food.
3. What would make you not trust a post?

## Wrap-up
- Anything else I should have asked?
- Who else should I talk to?

**Tips:** ask about past behavior, not hypotheticals. Don't pitch the app until the end. Write notes right after, in [insights-log.md](insights-log.md).
