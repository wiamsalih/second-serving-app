# Usability and accessibility test plan

**Participants:** 5 students, including at least one who uses a screen reader or magnification if possible.

## Tasks
1. "You're hungry and vegetarian. Find something you can eat and claim it."
2. "You just ran an event with 15 leftover sandwiches in Bloomberg 131. Post them."
3. "Check whether the food you claimed is still yours to pick up."

Record: success or failure, time on task, where they hesitated, and what they said.

## Accessibility checks
- Complete every task using only the keyboard.
- Run a screen reader (VoiceOver: Cmd+F5 on Mac) through the board and the post form.
- Check color contrast with a tool such as WebAIM's contrast checker or Figma's Stark plugin.
- Zoom the browser to 200% and confirm nothing breaks.
